## [v0.1.0] — 2026-05-19

### Added
- `contracts/PBOS-Template.md`: full 9-section collaboration agreement template.
- `contracts/Clause-Pack.md`: four drop-in clauses (Science definition, ownership split, publication, operational safeguards).
- `docs/Implementation-Guidance.md`: operational steps covering device boundary, export rules, fine-tuning pipelines, artifact hashing, audit, and incident response.
- `docs/Checklists.md`: role-specific checklists for counsel, engineering, and research teams.
- `examples/Example-Integration-Notes.md`: fictional end-to-end walkthrough of a PBOS collaboration including the fine-tuning edge case.
- `CONTRIBUTING.md`: contribution guidelines.
- `.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/language-proposal.yml`: structured issue template for clause proposals.

### Notes
- Initial public release accompanying the working paper by Bergemann, Ghili, and Mekel-Bobrov (2026).
