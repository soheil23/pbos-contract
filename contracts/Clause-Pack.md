# PBOS Clause Pack (Copy-Paste Friendly)

Use these short clauses to graft PBOS concepts into an existing agreement.

## A. Definition of “The Science” (core boundary)
“**The Science**” means mathematical descriptions, model architectures, algorithmic methods, computer programs,
and data-free visual exhibits developed in the Project, **provided they do not incorporate or depend on Company Data**.
For clarity, any **untrained** model (or a model trained only on public or synthetic data) is The Science; any **model
trained on Company Data** is **Trained Weights** and not The Science.

## B. Ownership split
- **Company** owns Company Data, Trained Weights, and any Work Product incorporating Company Data.
- **University** owns The Science. Company receives a non-exclusive, royalty-free, worldwide license to use The Science.

## C. Publication
University may publish The Science. Drafts containing Company Confidential Information will be provided to Company
at least 30 days before public disclosure to allow security review and, if requested, a patent filing hold not to exceed 90 days.

## D. Operational safeguards
All processing of Company Data occurs on Company-controlled systems. Exports are limited to The Science.
Parties will maintain artifact manifests and hashes to verify compliance. Company may conduct reasonable audits.
